= Get Ready for Lightwell: Patch to Production at the Speed of AI

This lab demonstrates how Red Hat Lightwell transforms open source vulnerability remediation from a weeks-long manual process into a continuous, policy-driven automated operation.

Step into the role of Dinesh, a platform engineer at a fictional financial services company, arriving to find that Lightwell has already processed 298 patches across 29 applications overnight. Work through the flagged high-risk items and observe the full remediation pipeline — from patch ingestion through AI-assisted review, build, multi-cluster deployment, and automated ticket closure.
